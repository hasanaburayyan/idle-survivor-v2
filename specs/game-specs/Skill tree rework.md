Here are the new skills what they do and their prerequisites this is the tutorial skill tree that will be with you til level 30.

- Scavenge Multiplier: 
	- 4 levels
	- 25% more scrap per level
	- prereq: none
- Unlock Parts: 
	- 1 levels
	- Give access to parts resource
	- prereq: Scavenge Multiplier (1 level)
- Parts Multiplier:
	- 4 levels
	- 25% more parts per level
	- prereq: Unlock Parts
- Unlock Metal: 
	- 1 levels
	- Give access to metal resource
	- prereq: Unlock Parts (1 level)
- Metal Multiplier:
	- 4 levels
	- 25% more metal per level
	- prereq: Unlock Metal
- Unlock Fabric: 
	- 1 levels
	- Give access to fabric resource
	- prereq: Unlock Metal (1 level)
- Fabric Multiplier:
	- 4 levels
	- 25% more fabric per level
	- prereq: Unlock Fabric
- Unlock Food: 
	- 1 levels
	- Give access to food resource
	- prereq: Unlock Fabric
- Food Multiplier:
	- 4 levels
	- 25% more Food per level
	- prereq: Unlock Food
- Unlock Meds: 
	- 1 levels
	- Give access to Meds resource
	- prereq: Unlock Food
- Meds Multiplier:
	- 4 levels
	- 25% more Meds per level
	- prereq: Unlock Meds
- Unlock Shelter:
	- 1 levels
	- Give access to Build Shelter Action
	- prereq: Scavenge Multiplier (level 1)
- Unlock Classes:
	- 1 level (free to unlock)
