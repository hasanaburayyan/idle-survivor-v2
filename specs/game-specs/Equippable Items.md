Overview:
Every equippable item has a template from which it is created. It will have a "Slot", which Slot in the players "Equipment" menu that is occupies. "Effect" is the stat or set of stats that a piece of Equipment targets, and increases or decreases. "Power" is a PER EFFECT stat that applies to how much it alters the listed stat. Here is some example pieces of gear:

Bag:
Possible effects: Click Resource Yield 1% - 999%, Increased Group Yield(increases how much the user benefits from others) 1% - 999%, Automated Resource Yield 1% - 999%
Slot: Back

Handgun:
Possible Effects: Damage Dealt in Defensive Battle +1 - +20, Extra Health in Defensive Battle, +10 - +99
Slot: Tool

Armor Vest:
Possible Effects: Extra Health in Defensive Battle +20 - +199, Damage Taken in Defensive Battle per Option -1 - -8

The exact power should be determined by several things, including features that have not yet been implemented. For now, just give Armory a cost across all 6 resources, and give it an upgrade button, and the Armory level will determine the average result of crafting an item.
