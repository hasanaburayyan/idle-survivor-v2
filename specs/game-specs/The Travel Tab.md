
## Activity:
We're canonizing the concept of Activities, with limited usage and or costs. Some activities are resource gain, some are expenditure, and some are both. They are invisible until unlocked. Activities can have prerequisites in the form of skill tree nodes, and more in future iterations. The two we currently have, Scavenge and Build Shelter, have no prerequisites and are unlocked by default.
## Overview:
The first "Location" the player lands in, after account creation is The Wastes. We're going to add a new Activity to The Wastes, called "Build Shelter". It will have a button, like Scavenge, but instead of accruing resources, the player is going to spend them. We'll say it requires 1000 scrap, and it has a bar under it that grows until completion. Players can click to submit resources, in as much as 10% per click, as long as the player has the amount required. Once complete, the Activity will be removed from The Wastes, and the tab that reads "The Wastes" will transform into "Travel", with two locations: The Wastes and The Shelter.
## Location specific activities:
The Wastes is the only place you can "Build Shelter", until the Shelter is complete. Opening the Travel tab will replace the main screen region (Where Scavenge currently lives) with each location the player can travel to, maybe a little thematic icon, and a brief summary of what you do there. In the Shelter let's have a placeholder Activity that does nothing, called Build Workbench.

Each players current Location should be reported in the Social tabs as relevant.
