Overview:
The user can take a vote among their group to initiate a collaborative activity. Everyone in the group sees a notification, regardless of their current location and activity, and they. can vote Yay or Nay. If all members vote Yay, they are taken to a new screen inaccessible by default, where their group display cards are lined up on the left side of the screen, and zombie cards are presented on the right side of the screen. Each player sees their own instance of 3 options on screen. They will grab one option, and drag them onto a target of their choosing. Valid targets are their group members, and a zombie on the right side of the screen. The option resolves, and the player is immediately presented with another option. Each time a player resolves an option, they take damage equal to the number of zombies on screen.

If the players survive the wave, a new wave approaches, repeat until the players have all had their HP reduced to 0, and then they are shunted back to their previous location with a package of loot gifted to them.

Options:
Deal damage to a single target, randomized value between 1 and 8.
Deal damage to all zombies, randomized value between 1 and 4.
Heal a single ally to full HP.
Heal all allies a randomized value between 1 and 8.
Prevent the next instance of damage you and each member of your party would receive.
More options added as unlockables in future skills, structures, etc.

Zombies:
Random HP value between 1 and CURRENT WAVE.
Each zombie deals 1 damage by default, each time a player resolves an option.
